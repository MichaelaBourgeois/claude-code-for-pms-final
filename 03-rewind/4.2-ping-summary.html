1. Pings taken vs missed, by week

Taken held at 75–78% for six weeks. It fell on 4.2's release and has only partly recovered. The drop is in missed pings, not in turned-down ones.

The week of 10 Aug is split at 12 Aug: 10–11 Aug is before 4.2, 12–16 Aug is after.

2. It was not everyone equally: four responders collapsed

Farlight, The Undertow, Meteor Mite and Vesper went from ~49 pings a week combined to ~15, and 53–64% of the pings they still got were missed. Before 12 Aug they looked like everyone else.

3. What the data says, and what it doesn't

Supported by the data

  • Missed pings rose for all 16 responders, from 0–5% to 10–18% for the twelve and 53–64% for the four.
  • The four miss pings in their own home area too (40–67%), so distance from the callout does not explain them. Most others miss mainly on out-of-area pings.
  • Four different areas and four different handlers. Meteor Mite and The Gale share a handler (Kip) and area (Eastgate); The Gale is mostly fine.
  • Pings moved to other responders: ten gained volume.
  • 11% of callouts since 12 Aug have no recorded taker, vs 5.5% before.

Only a guess, still to confirm

  • The routing code treats a missed ping like a decline and penalizes it without decay, so misses push a responder down the order and they get fewer pings. This comes from reading the code. It is not tested.
  • The 60s wait (cut from 90s) raised misses for everyone. The timestamps show the wait changed, but response times for taken pings are not in the database.
